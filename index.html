Scott Street

I like to make things. I'm based in Helsinki and my hobbies are maps, websites, and spending time outdoors.

Projects

  • Mewmap is a JavaScript library for displaying interactive maps from MVT vector tiles, powered by SVG. It uses Maplibre/Mapbox styles, and works well for simple maps as a lightweight alternative to other libraries.
  • Pollen.party is a pollen forecast webapp for Europe, using data from the Finnish Meteorological Institute. I gave a talk about it at EMF 2024.
  • Parkki is an iOS app for checking the car parking space availablity at Helsinki park and ride locations. I created it for myself, but the app is available for download on the App Store.
  • Postipäivät is an iOS app which shows the next Finnish postal service delivery dates for a given postal code. This too was created for myself, and is also available on the App Store.
